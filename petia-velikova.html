Petia Velikova

Secure Systems Engineer · Technical Operations & Automation · Systems Architecture & Reliability

Through her experience, Petya has developed a broad understanding of how applications, infrastructure, data, and hardware interact, and what it takes to make these systems reliable, maintainable, and secure in real-world environments.

She believes good engineering goes beyond making things work. It means understanding underlying problems, making thoughtful architectural decisions, anticipating failures, and building maintainable long-term solutions.

She approaches complex systems from first principles, investigating technical challenges, understanding how different components fit together, and improving development workflows through automation and effective engineering practices.

Software Engineering Secure Systems Engineering Systems Automation Cloud Infrastructure DevOps Systems Architecture Security Standards & Compliance Hardware Reliability

Secure Systems Engineering — Secure software development, security controls, security standards, and compliance.

Technical Operations & Automation — Improving workflows, connecting systems, troubleshooting complex issues, and reducing repetitive work.

Systems Architecture & Reliability — Understanding dependencies across software, infrastructure, and hardware to build resilient, maintainable systems.

As AI continues to change how software is built, Petya is interested in integrating these tools into established engineering practices without compromising software quality, security, maintainability, or sound architectural decisions.

She sees AI as an opportunity to extend engineering capabilities while keeping critical thinking, technical judgment, and responsibility for the resulting systems at the center of the process.

She aims to exchange ideas, learn from different approaches, and be part of an environment that encourages thoughtful experimentation, craftsmanship, and practical engineering.

Ultimately, Petya enjoys turning complex technical ideas into useful systems and continuously improving how they are designed, built, secured, and operated.
